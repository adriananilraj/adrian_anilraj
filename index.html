The human layer

AI can generate screens. Engineering makes them dependable.

AI is already useful for exploration, scaffolding, and repetitive work. But production systems still depend on context, trade-offs, security, accountability, and people who understand what happens when something goes wrong.

01

Server-side decisions

Business rules, API contracts, permissions, error handling, background jobs, and integrations require deliberate design.

02

Storage and data

Engineers decide how information is structured, queried, retained, backed up, migrated, and recovered safely.

03

User identity and privacy

Authentication, authorisation, consent, validation, secrets, and sensitive user data cannot be treated as visual details.

04

Real-world judgement

Humans understand the business, challenge assumptions, test edge cases, make trade-offs, and own the outcome after launch.
